Add to Home Screen

Installed, the app runs full screen — and Safari keeps its photos safe. A website's storage is cleared after a week without a visit; an installed app's is not.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari — the square with an arrow coming out of it.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open Photo Points from your Home Screen.
  1. Open your browser's menu (⋮).
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

The installed app has its own storage: it starts empty and asks you to sign in once. Let anything waiting finish uploading here first.
