Run it full screen

Safari always keeps its address bar, and in landscape it eats a good part of a short screen. Installing the app to the Home Screen drops the browser chrome entirely — same app, same offline queue, no bar.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari — the square with an arrow coming out of it.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open Exotic Camera from your Home Screen.
  1. Open your browser's menu (⋮).
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iOS gives the installed app its own storage, separate from Safari's: it starts with an empty queue and asks you to sign in once. Let the queue finish uploading in Safari before you switch over.

Sign in to ArcGIS

Everything on this service needs a token, so each user signs in with their own ArcGIS account. Nothing but the resulting token is kept on the device.

Needs an app id in Settings. Preferred — the app never sees your password.
